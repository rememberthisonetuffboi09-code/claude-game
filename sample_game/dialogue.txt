=== VILLAGE OF OAKHOLLOW — DIALOGUE ===

INNKEEPER: Welcome, traveler! You look tired. A room is 10 gold.
BLACKSMITH: Bring me iron and I'll forge you something worthy.
OLD MAN: They say a great treasure sleeps beneath the mountain.
GUARD: Move along. Nothing to see here.

QUEST: Retrieve the Rusty Key from the goblin cave and return it to the Innkeeper.
