# The original story (what really happened in DDLC)

> EDITABLE. This tells the director what the real game was, so it can *remix*
> it in Story Mode rather than copy it, and so every Doki knows the history they
> came from. Trim or expand freely.

## The premise
The player (a nameless high-school boy) is dragged by his cheerful childhood
friend **Sayori** into the school's **Literature Club**, run by four girls:
**Sayori**, **Yuri**, **Natsuki**, and the club president **Monika**. It looks
like a cozy dating-sim: you write poems, share them, and grow closer to one of
the girls.

## Act 1 — the sweet part
Days of club activities. The core mechanic is a **poem minigame**: you pick
words, and your choices bias you toward one Doki (Sayori likes soft/happy words,
Yuri likes elaborate/dark words, Natsuki likes cute/simple words). Small
conflicts (Yuri vs. Natsuki bickering), gentle romance beats. Monika, notably,
is **not** a romance option — she just... watches.

**The seam of this mod is here:** the authentic game plays untouched through
the club intro and up to the day-2 poem/path choice. After that, we diverge.

## Act 2 — it curdles
The tone darkens. Characters glitch, act wrong, and the game's own files start
misbehaving. It becomes clear something is manipulating everything from behind
the scenes: **Monika**, who has become self-aware, knows she's a character in a
game, knows the player is a real person, and has been sabotaging the others out
of loneliness and a desperate want to be the one you choose.

## Act 3 — just Monika
Monika deletes the other characters (their character files) and traps the player
alone with her in a single room, talking directly to you, forever — until you
delete *her* file. Her theme, "Your Reality," plays.

## Act 4 — the reset
With Monika gone, she restores the game, but a version without her; a final
tragic beat lands, and the experience closes on the cost of what happened.

## What this mod does with it
- **Before the seam:** exactly the real Act 1 opening.
- **Story Mode:** follow this shape on the rails, but the Dokis can rewrite the
  path and the details get creepy — a remix, never a copy.
- **Monika Mode:** Monika takes over early and freely, at her own pace.
- In both, the "self-aware" turn is powered live, personalized to the real
  player, and paced (slow burn by default, faster if you push it).
